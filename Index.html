Graphic 01 · Governance Architecture

Multi-Tier Skill Governance Framework

Five tiers of aligned governance flowing seamlessly from apex policy to last-mile service delivery. Each institution performs clearly defined, complementary functions within the skill ecosystem.

Policy → Execution · 5-Tier Structure

Skill Governance: From Strategy to Service Delivery

🏛️
Tier 1 · Apex Policy Body
State Level Steering Council (SLSC)
Chaired by Hon'ble Chief Minister
Apex policy-making body providing strategic direction. Comprises Ministers, senior officials, industry representatives. Aligns skill development with Kerala's socio-economic priorities.
Policy Direction
⚙️
Tier 2 · Executive Convergence
High Power Committee (HPC)
Chaired by Chief Secretary · 9 members
Executive oversight and convergence mechanism. Facilitates inter-departmental coordination, resource optimisation, and implementation monitoring across all state departments.
Inter-Dept. Sync
🎓
Tier 3 · State Skill Secretariat
Kerala Academy for Skills Excellence (KASE)
State Skill Development Mission & Secretariat
Nodal agency for policy implementation, ecosystem development, curriculum governance, accreditation, and overall coordination. Manages BACs, CoEs, international partnerships, and the CAG platform.
Nodal Agency
🗺️
Tier 4 · Decentralised Planning
District Skill Committees (DSC)
14 Districts — Independent Labour Market Units
Principal planning body for demand & supply assessment, stakeholder convergence, and preparation of District Skill Development Plans (DSDPs). Integrates demand, supply, fund mobilisation, and infrastructure utilisation.
District Planning
📍
Tier 5 · Last-Mile Delivery
District Skill Development Centres (DSDCs)
One-Stop Skill Service Centres at District Level
Operational arm of the decentralised ecosystem. Handles beneficiary mobilisation, career guidance, training coordination, and direct delivery of skill services to students, unemployed youth, mid-career professionals, repatriates, and unorganised-sector workers.
